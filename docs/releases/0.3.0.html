Room 0.3.0

A new look, a newer engine, and a long list of fixes.

New

  • A redesigned sidebar in Liquid Glass: the sidebar toggle, your pinned extensions, Back and Forward, Reload, Downloads, your spaces and New Tab are glass buttons, and the page runs edge to edge.
  • Your tabs scroll under the sidebar's glass controls, blurred where they pass beneath.
  • A new mini-player: hover it for the artist, a progress bar you can click to seek, previous and next, mute and Picture in Picture.
  • Updates download on their own while automatic checks are on, and a banner offers to restart when one is ready.
  • Picture in Picture also starts when Room's window is covered by another app, and its window is rounded, with Room's own Liquid Glass controls: play and pause, skip 10 seconds, a scrubber, mute and Back to Tab. A call's window shows its controls around the call on hover.
  • Peek opens from the link you clicked, nearly full height, with the page behind it receding.
  • ⌃Tab shows a picture of each recent tab.
  • When the selected tab is scrolled out of view, a small pill with its icon takes you back to it.
  • The icon picker for spaces, folders and tabs has every emoji, searchable, and some 200 symbols.
  • Space settings are laid out like System Settings.
  • “Connection is secure” in the site panel shows the site's certificate.
  • DevTools docks right, bottom or left, or opens in its own window.
  • Crash reports, if you turn them on in Settings ▸ Privacy, go to Room's own server, not a third party.

Changed

  • Folders are one level deep. Folders you already nested stay as they are; imported subfolders merge into their folder.

Fixed

  • Delete works in the address bar when it has suggested the rest of an address.
  • Dragging tabs in the sidebar works again.
  • Buttons react when you press them.
  • The sidebar slides in and out, whether shown with ⌘S or revealed at the window's edge.
  • The extensions menu shows each extension's icon.
  • The first click on a folder opens it, even while its preview shows.
  • Long tab titles fade out everywhere, in split view and ⌃Tab too.
  • Five Essentials keep their size, with room for a sixth.
  • The downloads list is no longer see-through.
  • Split view keeps its shadows and the resize cursor.
  • Dragging at the top of a page no longer moves the window.
  • The audio icon no longer flickers during short pauses.

Engine

  • Chromium 157, with its security fixes.
